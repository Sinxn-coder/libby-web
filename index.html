THE LIFE OF A BOOK

The library doesn't stand still.

From the moment a new physical volume is cataloged to the second it is safely checked back onto the shelf, Liblet maintains absolute clarity.

01

Arrive & Catalog

A new book arrives. The librarian sticks a quick barcode on the back cover. It is immediately visible in the campus search.

02

Search & Reserve

A student searches from their phone, sees every copy is currently borrowed, and joins the fair reservation line with one click.

03

Return & Automatic Notice

As soon as the book is returned at the desk, Liblet safely sets it aside and alerts the waiting student that their copy is ready.

04

Easy Pickup

Hold timers only tick during library hours. The student presents their digital ID at the desk, collects the book, and starts reading.

INSTITUTIONAL INTEGRITY

Security and governance built into every layer.

Liblet respects the institutional custody of academic records. Access to library information is role-controlled, database operations are strictly audited, and member accounts are provisioned exclusively by authorized librarians.

Protected Campus Accounts

No open public signups. Member accounts are created and verified directly by campus librarians to keep data safe.

Strict Data Privacy

Borrowing history is private to each member. Students only see their own loans, protected by strict database-level security rules.

Clear Activity Records

Every book issued, returned, or renewed is permanently recorded, giving library staff complete operational clarity.

Transparent Guidelines

Policies reflect real institutional standards. Privacy practices and terms of service are published openly for all members.
